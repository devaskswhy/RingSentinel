RingSentinel — built for the Razorpay AI Buildathon, AI Risk Manager track. Coordinated fraud, seen as a graph.

Fraud rings are invisible one transaction at a time.

Card-testing, promo-farming, return-abuse — every order looks fine, alone. The coordination is in the relationship between accounts, not inside one payment.

Even Stripe Radar, Sift, or Razorpay’s own risk stack score one transaction at a time. That can’t see a ring — however good the model.

every one of these looks fine, alone

Fraud rings are invisible per transaction — card-testing, promo-farming, return-abuse. Even the best per-transaction tools, including Razorpay's own, can't see it, because coordination is between payments, not inside one.

The unit of detection is the cluster. Never the payment.

Everything else follows from this one decision.
